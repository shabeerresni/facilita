Who We Are

Facilita Firms Management LLC is a premium facility and hospitality management company headquartered in Dubai. We partner with world-class properties — from Burj Khalifa residences to Sheraton Hotels and Al Ghurair Centre — to deliver operational excellence.

Our Mission: To elevate the standard of facility management through innovation, sustainability, and an unwavering commitment to excellence across every asset we serve.
Our Vision: To be the most trusted name in facilities and hospitality management across the GCC, UK, and India — recognised for integrity, efficiency, and lasting value.
